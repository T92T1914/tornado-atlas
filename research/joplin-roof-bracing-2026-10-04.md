# Joplin: the roof and the walls it supported

This increment explains a building-performance question beside the existing hospital and school cases. At Home Depot, a roof could carry loads and provide sideways support for the concrete walls and interior frames. Losing it could therefore change the stability of more than the roof itself. The source photograph shows the resulting damage. The investigation supplies the interpretation of how that damage may have developed.
