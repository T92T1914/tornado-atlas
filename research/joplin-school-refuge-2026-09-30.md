# What the school refuge cases establish

The Joplin dossier now includes two school cases from the March 2014 NIST final report. They answer a narrower question than whether sheltering worked for everyone: what happened to the designated refuge areas, and what does the report actually know about their use?

## Source and inspection

The [NIST publication record](https://www.nist.gov/publications/final-report-national-institute-standards-and-technology-nist-technical-investigation) identifies NCSTAR 3 and its March 2014 final report. An agent read the text of Sections 3.3.2.1 and 3.3.2.2 and visually inspected PDF pages 274 to 277, printed pages 210 to 213, including Figures 3-123 and 3-124. The title page was also inspected.
