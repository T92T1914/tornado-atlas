Complete scan pixels were not visually inspected. No retained hash of image bytes, original print inspection or historical page revision is claimed.

The legacy rules reserve approval for specified uses and conditions on deposit in a repository. The current policy assigns responsibility for permission from the rights holder and for credit. An institutional statement that it retains rights does not independently identify either photograph's copyright owner. A reproduction offered without charge would not establish permission for public exhibit or for hosting in a repository. The archive also cautions that online scans can be distorted or incomplete.

Atlas therefore supplies source links and attributed caption paraphrase only. It creates no image derivative, embedded archive thumbnail, remote image request, scan copy or new media record. A future increment of hosted media needs a rights basis for each item and any required archive permission, followed by legitimate inspection of the complete pixels. The source's factual relevance does not settle reuse.

The chapter keeps both item identifiers visible, sends readers to the original gallery and provides separate routes to the observations and the source cards. All existing media, records, clocks, source rows and reconstruction limits remain intact. The addition is archive navigation, not acceptance of new documentary photograph pixels, surveyed damage or historical registration. The disposition of no replay remains unchanged.
