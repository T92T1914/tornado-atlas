# Recovery and remembrance after the Tuscaloosa tornado

The existing chapter follows damage, warning communication, radar, field surveys
and source disagreements. Its remembrance paragraph still identified local
commemoration as future research. This addition connects those accounts to
recovery planning and three municipal memorial places. It does not add media,
reconcile casualty totals or register historical appearance.

## What the addition supports

The County EMA distinguishes city planning from the Holt community coalition.
Its planning account covers Tuscaloosa and Holt. The three City accounts retain
the narrower Tuscaloosa place label and are not extended to Holt.
The City distinguishes the recovery area's administrative purpose from the
earlier damage assessment. Its memorial page distinguishes volunteers, first
responders and those who died. The dated announcement supplies a commemoration
clock, kept separate from the 2011 storm clocks.

The new article section links back to the estimated footprint, county survey,
source definitions and complete source cards. It remains ordinary HTML without
scripting. Source links are useful without loading a third-party dashboard.

The current dossier retains every earlier source, observation, media item,
creator, county association and historical limit. Four sources, four observations
and one documentary route are appended through the existing curator publication
path. No camera point, impact time, measured funnel width, full recovery claim
or new fatality total is inferred. The three municipal memorial places are not
death locations.

## Rights and verification

This is factual paraphrase with attributed external links. No city photograph,
dashboard, map, video or plaque text is hosted, embedded or adapted. Public page
access does not grant rights to its media.

The existing archive publisher retains the predecessor and writes a new content
addressed dossier, history and source directory. Focused controls check the exact
delta, source and clock roles, refusal of unsupported coordinates or alignment,
article navigation, and the absence of new media. Source inspection, local
checks, independent review, CI and served acceptance remain separate evidence.
