# Joplin: the school and hospital in aftermath photographs

Two photographs published in NWS Springfield's anniversary StoryMap add specific
views to the existing Joplin reading. The school photograph belongs beside the
NIST refuge account. The wider hospital photograph supplies the setting for the
care and services account, while the existing NIST photographs retain their
more specific explanations of the tower and windows.

## What each view can explain

The school image shows major roof and wall damage, exposed and collapsed
framing, rubble and debris. It does not identify the designated refuge areas
or establish their condition, occupancy or use. The finding that those areas
had no structural damage comes from the separate NIST investigation, which
also preserves its missing occupancy evidence. The agency's adjacent bus
account is not a visible feature of this selected photograph.

The hospital image shows damaged windows and roof-level structures behind
wrecked and overturned vehicles and debris. It supplies wider aftermath
context. It cannot establish the condition of internal services, generator
failure, the order of failures or an occupant outcome. NIST's findings about
structure, enclosure and function remain separately attributed.

These views are not a controlled comparison of construction or wind exposure.
Neither is a wind measurement, timed collapse record or tornado-body image.
