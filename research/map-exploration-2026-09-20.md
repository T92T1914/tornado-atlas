# Exploring the maps and expanding the El Reno report

## What changed

The atlas, historical path and damage survey now share pointer-centered wheel
zoom, dragging, pinch gestures and keyboard navigation. Zoom buttons remain
available. A drag does not select the point underneath it. Ctrl plus wheel
remains available for browser zoom. Path points now seek the timeline to their
published time, and their size stays readable as the viewport or map scale
changes.

Catalogue cards use the selected reading appearance. Rating labels use the
normal text color with a separate colored swatch. This fixes both the green
Noir cards and pale rating text on Light backgrounds.

The damage survey has a separate rose diamond for the documented TWISTEX
vehicle recovery location. Opening it names Tim Samaras, Paul Samaras and Carl
Young, links the research account and explains the location limits. Damage
rating and photograph filters do not filter that account. A recovery location
does not establish an exact place of death, and no injury locations have been
invented. The source and coordinates are the previously documented record in
the remembrance data, not a new geolocation result.

## Research added to the report

Five sections extend the earlier six: formation in radar, smaller vortices,
field damage, the later flooding, and how to read films alongside the evidence.
A contents list links all eleven sections.

The claim ledger, reading list and video review queue record these distinctions.
The transcript is not republished. New historical paragraphs are summaries with
source links, not copied article passages.

## What remains

The interaction changes do not add a street or satellite basemap, establish
new death locations, register documentary footage to the map, or validate the
historical appearance and damage models. Each of those needs its own evidence
and implementation. Physical mobile devices, Firefox, Safari and arbitrary
theme extensions have not been checked for this update.
