# Pressure and debris in the Blackwell account

This increment answers a visitor question left open by the damage map and conflicting clocks: what can a pressure record or fallen tree actually establish? It adds a reading section and two attributed observations using the existing source model. It does not create another source register, a pressure chart, a new track or a wind reconstruction.

## Exact source and inspection

The source is the [NWS Wichita reproduction of the May 25, 1955 storm report](https://www.weather.gov/ict/udall_stormreport). The Tonkawa and Blackwell sections were read again through the supported text interface on October 5, 2026 UTC. The full report was already included in the earlier source inspection. This addition uses the existing `nws-wichita` source identity. The current reproduction has not been checked against a complete original printed scan, and its named historical observers are not treated as the authors or current uploaders of the report.

The Tonkawa passage reports that Mrs. Robert C. Walker had a micro-barograph operating when a funnel was seen approximately one mile east of town. It describes a pressure fall of about 0.08 inch Hg followed immediately by a rise of about 0.10 inch Hg. The minimum's approximate label is 2055 CST, accompanied by an explicit lack of instrument time checks. These remain reported pressure differences in their original units. No raw trace, calibration, exact station location, UTC conversion or event synchronization was obtained. A nearby observation does not establish pressure at a Blackwell building or reconcile the Blackwell clock labels.

The Blackwell passage describes trees west of the path blown eastward and trees east of it blown westward. The report interprets this as inflow, with less clear evidence of rotation, and says the origin of building debris was difficult to determine. Those are attributed qualitative survey observations. They do not supply measured wind speed, building coordinates, a complete vector field or collapse timing. The chapter does not turn this historical interpretation into a validated mechanism.
