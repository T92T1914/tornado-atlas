# Radar above Joplin's damage layer

The new radar reading adds a different kind of evidence beside Joplin's storm photographs, warning chronology and building cases. Visitors can compare seven paired radar snapshots, read the velocity and reflectivity panels correctly, and see why a radar image was not a direct wind measurement at a damaged building.

## Reading the seven pairs

Each snapshot places radial velocity on the left and reflectivity on the right. The printed UTC labels are 2224, 2229, 2234, 2239, 2243, 2248 and 2253 on May 22, 2011. The figure is a selection of observations rather than every radar update during that interval.

The caption explains approaching and receding velocity colors relative to the Springfield radar, east of Joplin. In the 2248 UTC pair, NIST highlights adjacent opposite radial velocities and a hook echo in reflectivity. Those features support its interpretation of rotation and approximate tornado position. The color scales and annotation are preserved. No wind speed, building coordinate, funnel outline or optical appearance is measured from this rendering.

NIST reports that the relevant distant radars were approximately 100 kilometers from the storm. Even their lowest beam elevation sampled roughly 1.5 kilometers above the ground. Building damage occurred much closer to the surface. The report therefore used indirect methods to estimate near-surface winds, including inference from observed damage and a wind model fitted to tree falls. Surveyed outcomes, measurements aloft and modeled winds answer related but different questions. This addition does not republish that wind model or validate it independently.

Printed page 28 pairs 5:38 p.m. with 2258 UTC, which is inconsistent with the conversion used for the surrounding labels. That discrepancy is retained as a source limit. The existing NWS-based chronology is unchanged, and no new alignment is assigned from that pairing.

The dossier records the figure as source-reported radar evidence, with source clock labels, unregistered spatial context and permitted hosting under the selected basis. It adds no camera, map point, derived alignment, continuous playback interval or historical appearance reconstruction. Every prior source, media, observation, creator, route and record association is retained.

Source and data checks cover the derivative identity, preserved earlier dossier rows, separate attribution, raw-data and model limits, and rejection of unsupported spatial coordinates.
