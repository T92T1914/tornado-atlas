## Interpretation and disagreement

Two independently authored cubic Bezier curves were mathematically sampled at
17 points each. The points were authored from qualitative direction and broad
bend, without reading or tracing source raster bytes. One common isotropic
display plane and identical variant parameters were used at each reported
instant. Four variant captures therefore represent two optical exposures.

The simpler curve is the conservative default. Its open descending-right margin
expresses the photographs' coarse direction and broad bend. The stronger
S-shaped variant is retained as an authored alternative. That specific smooth
inflection is not uniquely established by both photographs. Neither curve
matches fine scallops, local irregularities, the complete dark mass, foreground
features or a complete silhouette.

The independent comparison accepted this limited interpretation. It did not
accept a measured photographic contour or calibrated projection. Repeating one
interpretation at two labels recovers no growth, change or intervening motion.
The upper and lower continuations, opposite margin, surface contact, depth,
physical dimensions and numeric camera fields remain unknown.
