THIS HAZARDOUS WEATHER OUTLOOK COVERS NORTHERN...WESTERN...
CENTRAL...AND SOUTHERN OKLAHOMA...AND WESTERN NORTH TEXAS.

.DAY ONE...TODAY AND TONIGHT...

THUNDERSTORM OUTLOOK...
THERE IS A RISK FOR SEVERE STORMS ACROSS PARTS OF NORTH CENTRAL...
CENTRAL... AND SOUTHERN OKLAHOMA... AND WESTERN NORTH TEXAS THROUGH
TONIGHT.

LOCATION...
THE MODERATE RISK IS ACROSS CENTRAL AND SOUTHERN OKLAHOMA... SOUTH
OF A LINE FROM STILLWATER AND GUTHRIE...TO EL RENO AND FORT COBB...
AND EAST OF A LINE FROM FORT COBB... TO LAWTON AND WALTERS... THEN
NORTH OF A LINE FROM WALTERS AND DUNCAN... TO SULPHUR AND ADA... TO
HOLDENVILLE OKLAHOMA. THE SLIGHT RISK IS OUTSIDE THE MODERATE RISK
AND SOUTH AND EAST OF A LINE FROM BLACKWELL AND WATONGA... TO CLINTON
AND ALTUS OKLAHOMA... TO KNOX CITY TEXAS.

IMPACTS...
THE STRONGEST STORMS WILL BE CAPABLE OF PRODUCING HAIL UP TO THE
SIZE OF SOFTBALLS... WIND GUSTS OF 75 MPH... AND TORNADOES. HEAVY
RAINFALL LEADING TO FLOODING WILL ALSO BE POSSIBLE.

TIMING...
THE MOST LIKELY TIME FOR SEVERE STORMS WILL BE BETWEEN 4 PM AND
MIDNIGHT.

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS...
STORM SPOTTER GROUPS AND EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT PERSONNEL ACROSS
THE RISK AREA SHOULD BE PREPARED FOR POSSIBLE SEVERE WEATHER
OPERATIONS LATE THIS AFTERNOON AND THIS EVENING.

DISCUSSION... 
A FRONTAL BOUNDARY IS EXPECTED TO EXTEND FROM WEST CENTRAL TO NORTH
CENTRAL OKLAHOMA BY MID-AFTERNOON... WITH A DRYLINE INTERSECTING THE
FRONT IN WESTERN OKLAHOMA AND EXTENDING SOUTHWARD INTO TEXAS. A CAP
WILL LIKELY PREVENT THUNDERSTORM DEVELOPMENT UNTIL LATE THIS AFTERNOON.
THE CAP IS EXPECTED TO WEAKEN LATE THIS AFTERNOON WITH CONVERGENCE
ALONG THE BOUNDARY AND SURFACE HEATING PROVIDING LIFT FOR THUNDERSTORM
DEVELOPMENT. THE AIRMASS AHEAD OF THESE BOUNDARIES WILL BE WARM AND
MOIST BECOMING VERY UNSTABLE THIS AFTERNOON. THE STRONG INSTABILITY
AND FAVORABLE WIND SHEAR WILL LEAD TO SEVERE STORMS LATE THIS AFTERNOON
AND EVENING. VERY LARGE HAIL... DAMAGING WINDS... AND TORNADOES WILL
BE POSSIBLE WITH THE SEVERE STORMS. THE POTENTIAL FOR TORNADOES WILL
BE GREATEST FROM 5 PM TO 9 PM AS THE WIND SHEAR INCREASES ACROSS THE
AREA. THUNDERSTORMS WILL CONTINUE OVERNIGHT INTO EARLY SATURDAY MORNING
BUT THE SEVERE POTENTIAL WILL DIMINISH LATE THIS EVENING.  

PROBABILITY TABLE...
VALID THROUGH 700 AM CDT SATURDAY JUN 1.
PROBABILITY OF THUNDERSTORMS OCCURRING IN THE
              NWS NORMAN COUNTY WARNING AREA...100 PERCENT.
PROBABILITY OF SEVERE STORMS IF STORMS OCCUR...100 PERCENT.

OTHER HAZARDOUS WEATHER...
HEAVY RAINFALL COULD LEAD TO FLASH FLOODING THIS AFTERNOON INTO
SATURDAY AS THE FRONT SLOWLY MOVES SOUTH INTO THE VERY MOIST AIRMASS.
RAINFALL AMOUNTS OF 2 TO 4 INCHES COULD BE POSSIBLE IN SOME LOCATIONS.
WITH ALREADY SATURATED GROUNDS IN SOME AREAS... THIS HEAVY RAIN COULD
LEAD TO FLASH FLOODING. A FLASH FLOOD WATCH IS IN EFFECT FOR PARTS OF
NORTH CENTRAL AND CENTRAL OKLAHOMA.

BUT THE POTENTIAL FOR HEAVY RAINFALL WILL CONTINUE.
