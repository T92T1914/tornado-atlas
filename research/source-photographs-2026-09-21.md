# Two original El Reno photographs

Reviewed September 21, 2026. This extends the existing unregistered observation
notebook. It does not register a new historical camera or appearance reconstruction.

## Source and inspected coverage

[William T. Hark's May 31 account](https://www.harkphoto.com/05312013.html)
was read, including the selected captions and rights notice. The
[El Reno Survey Project participants list](https://el-reno-survey.net/participants/),
row 36, identifies Bill Hark and links that account. Its displayed last update
is October 23, 2015. This corroborates the account's place in that collection,
not the accuracy of its clock or camera location.

the event date and image copyright year are not publication dates.

## What the records support

The caption is the source of the named place and minute label. A displayed
minute does not establish a 30-second error bound, clock calibration, an exact
UTC time or a precise viewing direction. The stills do not establish motion,
funnel dimensions, a circulation boundary or the tornado's changing wind field.
The enhancement statement is especially relevant when interpreting contrast.

Both records retain `historical_utc: null` and `camera_position: null`.
They do not seek the shared clock, add map pins, infer a fatality location or
fill gaps in the registered appearance evidence. Original sources can change;
these are dated inspection records, not preserved image-byte snapshots.

## Rights and implementation

The account states copyright 2013 William T. Hark and offers image licensing.
Only credited original-photo and account links are published here. No source
photograph, thumbnail, video or transcript is mirrored or embedded.

`observations.json` remains the source of truth, validated by
`atlas.observations.validate_notebook` and copied by the existing exhibit build.
The optional photograph collection requires provenance, inspected coverage,
separate visual and caption notes, explicit unknown registration, processing
status and links-only rights. Unsupported exact placement and silent extra
registration fields fail validation. The interface remains useful without
external image loading; opening an original source is the reader's choice.

Next evidence need: independently inspect clock calibration and original
camera-location evidence before proposing any historical registration. A
precisely drawn marker cannot substitute for those missing observations.
