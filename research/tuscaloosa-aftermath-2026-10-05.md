# A neighborhood photograph with its limits intact

links one image as "Destruction just north of Birmingham." The complete
[6.JPG](https://www.weather.gov/images/bmx/significant_events/2011/042711/tuscbirm/6.JPG)
shows damaged or missing roofs, debris across streets and yards, and damaged
trees among standing structures. It helps the visitor connect the written
damage account to the scale of a neighborhood. It does not identify the
particular buildings described at McDonald Chapel.

## Source and reuse decision

Two agents inspected the complete original 800 by 600 image on October 5, 2026
UTC. Its pixels include the agency credit `NWS BMX` and the printed date
`04/29/2011`. The individual photographer is unnamed. The exact linked survey
caption contains no separate third-party credit or restriction. The surrounding
survey assistance acknowledgments do not identify this photograph's creator.

The [NWS use policy](https://www.weather.gov/disclaimer), inspected on the same
date, supplies a public-domain default for agency material unless otherwise
marked and separately reserves third-party products. The embedded agency mark
and exact official context support applying that default to this one image.
This is an item-specific inference, not proof of the photographer's employment
or permission to host every image on the page. Attribution remains visible and
no government endorsement is implied.
