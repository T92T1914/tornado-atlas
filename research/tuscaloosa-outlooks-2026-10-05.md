# Forecast language before the tornado

This contribution adds one comparison linked to its source to the existing
Tuscaloosa warning chapter. It shows regional forecast language before the
afternoon tornado, alongside the later assessment's findings on warning
management and the last mile. Forecast issuance, tornado arrival and warning
receipt remain separate.

## Primary source and inspected portions

[NWS Birmingham's compilation of Hazardous Weather Outlooks issued before the event](https://www.weather.gov/bmx/event_04272011hwo)
was inspected as accessible official text on October 5, 2026. The selected
headers and corresponding threat paragraphs are:

| Compilation section | Printed issue clock | Portion inspected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friday, April 22 | 6:49 a.m. CDT, April 22, 2011 | Header and Days Two Through Seven paragraph |
| Tuesday, April 26 | 5:54 a.m. CDT, April 26, 2011 | Header and Wednesday threat paragraph |
| Wednesday, April 27 | 3:19 a.m. CDT, April 27, 2011 | Header, Day One threat and preparation paragraphs |

The first selected outlook increases confidence for Wednesday while retaining
low Tuesday confidence. The next describes likely afternoon and evening
supercells capable of strong tornadoes with long tracks. The April 27 text
describes a likely outbreak of severe weather and possible strong tornadoes with
long lifespans, urging preparation and monitoring while retaining timing
uncertainty. These paraphrases describe the agency's forecasts, not subsequently
measured behavior.

The page is a present official compilation reproducing historical bulletin text.
Its own original publication and revision dates are not established. Original
bulletin transmission files were not independently matched, and no complete
original bulletin byte hash is claimed. A retained current HTML identity, if

## Rights, clocks and spatial limits

original source. It does not host a screenshot, photograph, map, copied webpage
design or asset from a third party. The existing record for NWS government
material and its exceptions for third parties remains applicable without
clearing other visual items by association. The museum does not imply agency
