warning chapter. It shows regional forecast language before the afternoon
tornado, alongside the later assessment's warning-management and last-mile
findings. Forecast issuance, tornado arrival and warning receipt remain separate.

was inspected as accessible official text on October 5, 2026. The selected
headers and corresponding threat paragraphs are:

| Compilation section | Printed issue clock | Portion inspected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friday, April 22 | 6:49 a.m. CDT, April 22, 2011 | Header and Days Two Through Seven paragraph |
| Tuesday, April 26 | 5:54 a.m. CDT, April 26, 2011 | Header and Wednesday threat paragraph |
| Wednesday, April 27 | 3:19 a.m. CDT, April 27, 2011 | Header, Day One threat and preparation paragraphs |

The first selected outlook increases confidence for Wednesday while retaining
low Tuesday confidence. The next describes likely afternoon and evening
supercells capable of strong, long-tracked tornadoes. The April 27 text describes
a likely severe-weather outbreak and possible strong, long-lived tornadoes,
urging preparation and monitoring while retaining timing uncertainty. These
paraphrases describe the agency's forecasts, not subsequently measured behavior.
