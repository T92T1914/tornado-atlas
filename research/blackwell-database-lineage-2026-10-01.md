# Blackwell's historical database lineage

NWS Norman's Blackwell event page says its historical tornado listing incorporates changes described in Doug Speheger's *Corrections to the Historic Tornado Database*, NOAA Technical Memorandum NWS SR-209, February 2001. That reference explains how the listing was prepared. It is not an additional eyewitness account of Blackwell.

## What the original report supports

The report describes a review of tornado records from 1950 to 1995 for the Norman forecast area. For years before Storm Data began in 1959, it used climatological publications and local records. Its web listing replaced coordinate tracks with descriptions relative to towns. Clear errors or sufficiently supported discrepancies were changed. Unresolved disagreements could remain in the database. These methods appear in Sections 2 and 3, printed pages 1 to 2, corresponding to PDF pages 3 to 4.

Section 4C, printed pages 4 to 5, distinguishes the path of a storm producing a family of tornadoes from an individual continuous tornado path. Section 4D and the conclusion, printed pages 6 to 7, delimit the review. F-scale ratings were not revised, many length and width differences were not investigated, and the review stops at 1995. These are qualifications on this particular review, not a present-day accuracy estimate for every tornado database.

This addition does not identify a Blackwell-specific correction in the inspected passages. It does not reconcile the chapter's clock labels, injury counts or differing path lengths. Atlas retains the existing source-record association and unresolved values.
