# El Reno community discussions

Reviewed September 20, 2026.

The exhibit now keeps three curated discussions outside the factual history.
Each entry has a claim, source check, conclusion, unresolved work and review
date. Source roles distinguish a community argument from a primary record.
These labels describe our review, not a vote or a claim of expert consensus.

The complete citation trail and access notes are in
[community.json](../exhibits/el-reno-2013/community.json). All three Reddit posts
and the comment text returned by the browser research service were inspected.
Collapsed or omitted replies are outside that coverage. No comments, images or
user profiles were copied into the exhibit, and no messages were posted.

## Selection and checks

* The rating thread shows competing interpretations. The NWS event account,
  scale explanation and selected sections of the survey paper provide the
  evidence check. The discussion does not change the official record.
* The house thread supplied a specific construction lead. Section 4.2 and
  Figure 13's caption in the survey paper support that detail. The rest of the
  thread's damage assertions were not all independently checked.
* The width thread links to Matthew Gove's September 2013 article. Its text was
  read, but the research paper it describes remains unidentified. We have not
  verified its image tables or adopted commenters' explanations for the larger
  number. This is an unresolved source trail, not a confirmed alternative width.

The primary paper is a 2014 AMS conference manuscript by Marshall and
colleagues. Selected sections were read, not every figure or page continuously.
The source URL and precise coverage are stored in the exhibit data. Its Figure
1 also offers a promising timed ground-photo lead for future registration;
reuse rights and camera geometry still need work before a local reproduction.

## Editorial rules for the next exhibits

Community posts, videos and eyewitness accounts can contribute different kinds
of evidence. A creator's original recording can establish a visible feature
within the inspected shot; the creator's rating opinion is a separate claim.
Record the video timestamp, historical-clock alignment, viewpoint, edits and
inspection coverage. Do not attribute a statement to a creator because a
commenter says they made it. A transcript alone does not verify the image.

Follow specific leads to survey reports, original footage, measurement methods
or identifiable eyewitness testimony. Track disagreement and access failures.
Several posts repeating one source are one evidence stream. Research should
include local history and overlooked observations as well as rating disputes.
Do not promote a claim merely because it sounds plausible or is often repeated.

Keep useful uncertainty visible. Correct the factual history only when the
supporting record warrants it, preserving the previous claim and reason for
revision. The source validation code checks citation structure and access
status; it cannot establish that an editorial conclusion is scientifically true.

## Software checks

Six tests cover collection integrity, wrong-event data, unsupported conclusion
labels, forum-only evidence, missing or unread citations, duplicate references,
unsafe URLs and review dates. Discussion rendering uses text nodes and curated
HTTPS links. No discussion text becomes HTML or executable instructions.
