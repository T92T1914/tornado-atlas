# Moore radar animations: source and clock comparison

The NWS Norman event account links two KTLX radar animations for the May 20, 2013 Newcastle and Moore storm. One shows reflectivity and the other shows storm relative velocity. They are different products from the same radar, rather than independent optical viewpoints. The complete source files are retained unchanged. [NWS event account](https://www.weather.gov/oun/events-20130520).

Each GIF contains 24 presentations at 800 by 558 pixels, with a stored delay of 350 milliseconds per presentation. The resulting 8.4 second display cycle is an animation setting, not elapsed historical time or a measured browser playback duration. Native browser decoding produced 24 distinct RGBA outputs for each product. Full presentations 0, 12 and 23 were inspected from both files. Contact sheets cover all presentations at reduced review size. This is not a full size visual inspection of every frame.

The source page describes a range from 3:04 PM to 4:42 PM CDT. The sampled VST labels read 19:04:13Z, 19:55:30Z and 20:42:18Z. The beginning and ending labels differ by one hour from the page range under the ordinary CDT conversion. Atlas preserves both statements. Neither is silently corrected, used to infer a camera offset or aligned with the historical replay. The product panels also contain separate Prod fields, which are retained without substituting them for VST.

The complete color scales, map labels, KTLX label and product panels remain visible. No third party copyright or courtesy credit was visible in the inspected full presentations and contact sheets. Item reuse rests on the identified agency radar products and the [NWS information use policy](https://www.weather.gov/disclaimer). It does not extend to the separately credited tornado photographs on the event page. No government endorsement is implied.

The radar section begins with a complete decoded first presentation as a separate PNG poster. Choosing play loads the original GIF. Stop removes the animated image and restores the first presentation. This is a stop control, not an exact frame pause. Without scripts, the poster, original GIF and source links remain available. Article interaction, error recovery, CI and public release acceptance are separate from this source qualification.
