# El Reno fatality locations and photograph context

Reviewed September 21, 2026. This pass covers Richard Charles Henderson's
location leads and the connection between fatality cards and survey photographs.
It does not establish a new fatality coordinate.

## Henderson

His [published obituary](https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/oklahoman/name/richard-henderson-obituary?id=22636764)
confirms his name, age 35 and death on May 31, 2013. It supplies no incident
coordinate. [National Geographic's reporting](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/magazine/article/the-last-chase)
describes a recovery near a creek roughly a mile south of the TWISTEX wreck.
That is a relative recovery description, not a unique location or a fatal
impact point.

The [Stormtrack discussion](https://stormtrack.org/threads/el-reno-question.27366/)
contains competing road and vehicle claims. Post 3 suggests Reno Road east of
US 81. Post 13 discusses SW 15th Street using a television report and vehicle
appearance. The [final reply, post 16](https://stormtrack.org/threads/el-reno-question.27366/page-2),
challenges that television account and links a contemporary survivor story.
The old NewsOK story did not load in this review. I recorded the challenge but
did not treat the inaccessible story as verified evidence of another location.

The [NWS event account](https://www.weather.gov/oun/events-20130531) places the
eight deaths in vehicles and describes road areas. It does not pair Henderson's
name with a precise coordinate. The existing five unlocated victims retain
their individual research accounts. No new coordinates have been assigned to
them in this pass.

A useful next source would be a public incident or recovery record, or an
independently corroborated eyewitness account that identifies Henderson and
distinguishes impact from recovery. Unnamed vehicle symbols, similar wreckage
and estimates copied between discussions do not establish that association.

## Photograph navigation

The TWISTEX recovery diamond previously required another click on the nearby
gray survey point to see DAT 165661's photograph. The fatality card now includes
that photograph directly. Both the card and enlarged viewer explain that it is
linked by location, not a confirmed identification of the people or vehicle.
The original NWS assessment and source link remain attached. No rating or
photograph attribution has been changed.

The reusable relationship uses survey points with photographs within 250 meters,
ordered by distance. This radius supports navigation; it is not an uncertainty
radius for the incident or proof of photographic identity. Damage filters do
not suppress evidence in an already selected fatality card.
