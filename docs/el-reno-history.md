# El Reno history and remembrance

I want the history to include the people affected, alongside the path and the
storm's appearance. The first exhibit now connects seven short chapters to the
published timeline, adds two credited photographs of the tornado, and records
the names of the eight people reported killed by it. Each name has a public
source. The section says "Rest in peace" without adding invented biographies,
portraits, or details about surviving relatives.

## Counts and scope

The displayed eight direct deaths and 26 direct injuries come from NOAA NCEI
Storm Events record 453682, snapshot `ncei:2013:20260323`. The annual source is
linked in the exhibit and its hash is retained. These are the published
record's values. They are not a fresh investigation or a claim that other
reported totals have been reconciled.

Contemporary CNN reporting, syndicated by FOX6, reports 115 people treated by
Oklahoma City area hospitals. That is a different reporting scope. It cannot
be substituted for the tornado's recorded injury count without further work.
The same evening's flooding caused additional deaths; the NWS regional flood
total is shown separately. "Casualties" and "wounded" are not extra numbers
to add to deaths and injuries.

## Sources read on September 20, 2026

* [NWS Norman event account](https://www.weather.gov/oun/events-20130531): historical context and chronology. The narrative and final mapped position end at 6:42 PM; the table and NCEI record give 6:44 PM. Both remain visible.
* [NOAA statement about the researchers](https://www.weather.gov/news/130603-chasers): Tim Samaras, Paul Samaras and Carl Young.
* [CNN report syndicated by FOX6](https://www.fox6now.com/news/breakdown-of-the-storm-fatalities-in-oklahoma): names and a wider storm fatality list attributed to the medical examiner. This early report contains superseded rating information and inconsistent ages, which are not repeated.
* [Juliana Keeping / The Oklahoman, syndicated by EMS World](https://www.hmpgloballearningnetwork.com/site/emsworld/news/11031041/language-barriers-hinder-storm-warnings-okla): Maria Pol Martin and Rey Chicoj Pol, with the El Reno tornado identified in the account.
* [Jones-Harkins Funeral Home obituary via Tribute Archive](https://www.tributearchive.com/obituaries/26979897/dustin-bridges): Dustin Heath Bridges and the date and place of death.
* [William O'Neal obituary at Legacy](https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/oklahoman/name/william-oneal-obituary?id=11764894): only a search-indexed excerpt was accessible in this pass. It is supplementary; the contemporary reporting is also linked beside his name.
* Both Wikimedia Commons photograph pages linked in `THIRD_PARTY.md`: photographer, license, source file and original dimensions checked. Both images were visually inspected. No capture time or viewing direction has been registered.

This is an independently assembled remembrance. It is not an official or
family-approved memorial. Names from the separate flooding disaster have not
yet been catalogued. Adding another storm requires its own records and source
review, rather than copying this exhibit's totals or assuming the same level
of coverage throughout the atlas.

## Software checks

`atlas.history.validate_history` rejects duplicate or unsourced names,
negative counts, chapters outside the published minute range, and changed
photograph bytes. The exhibit checker also compares the browser bundle with
the curated source files. These checks catch data mistakes; they cannot prove
a historical statement simply because a URL is present.
