`docs/reconstruction-plan.md`, the four current event dossiers, the fourteen
principal page sources and the El Reno reading records. An independent source
review inspected an immutable supplied packet. That review did not run the site
or inspect original media pixels.

An isolated Chromium walkthrough measured fourteen principal route states,
five narrow no-script entrances and a controlled exhibit-data failure. Four
baseline viewport captures were inspected. This was an expert walkthrough,
not a whole-page inspection of every state, physical-phone testing or feedback
from actual visitors. Recent public repository discussions inspected in this
pass supplied no new human visitor findings. No claim of improved comprehension
or engagement is made without such evidence.

## Reference observations and research

The [Smithsonian Searchable Museum](https://www.searchablemuseum.com/) supplies
a useful reference for discoverable exhibits and supporting resource routes.
[Apollo 13 in Real Time](https://apolloinrealtime.org/13/) illustrates purposeful
connections among a clock, photographs and documentary accounts. Their source
text was inspected. Their complete rendered interfaces and players were not
tested in this review. Neither reference supplies calibration for Atlas.

The [El Reno encyclopedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_El_Reno_tornado)
was a reference for headings, contents and further reading, not a replacement
for primary historical evidence. Atlas preserves the NWS account's separately
reported 6:42 and 6:44 endings. A Tornado Wiki route was unavailable to the
research tool because of its robots restriction and was not inspected.

[Walsh and colleagues' National Museums Liverpool study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00799-018-0248-8)
supports serving different visitor motivations and making navigation useful
alongside search. Its original article, methods and limitations were read. The
survey was self-selected and belongs to a different institution. It cannot
establish how Atlas visitors behave.

[Lushnikova and colleagues' museum website study](https://orbilu.uni.lu/handle/10993/55773)
offers a related hypothesis about understandable choices, motivation and
engagement. Only the institutional abstract and indexed method excerpts were
inspected here. The complete paper was not read. These references motivate
design choices; actual Atlas comprehension remains an unanswered question.

## Historical distinctions to preserve

- **El Reno:** final EF3 damage rating, early EF5 media titles, reported 2.6-mile
  maximum, visible condensation and the wider aerial damage envelope describe
  different things. Keep all eight named tornado deaths, separately reported
  flood deaths and differently scoped injury and hospital figures. The vehicle
  recovery marker is not an exact death site or time.
- **Joplin:** whole path and strongest-damage extent differ. Keep contemporary
  direct and indirect counts separate from later remembrance. The selected
  warning chronology does not establish the tornado's ending or each person's
  warning receipt. A standing hospital frame does not establish usable services.
- **Blackwell:** retain conflicting injury figures, reported whole path versus
  county segment, and all source clock labels. Tonkawa's unchecked barograph
  clock cannot reconcile Blackwell. Do not turn caption-only archival leads
  into inspected or permitted photographs.
- **Tuscaloosa and Birmingham:** retain the whole-track and county scopes,
  local damage ratings, approximate endpoints, different fatality definitions
  and source intervals. Satellite products and contextual aftermath photographs
  have different extents, dates and evidence roles.

## The first release and its remaining evidence

The implementing candidate is intended to supply a complete four-exhibit
entrance, consistent navigation, recognizable event headings, resilient El Reno
reading and permitted responsive photographs. It preserves the historical
renderer, established URLs and original documentary evidence.

Release acceptance requires an immutable candidate, consequential independent
review, actual local checks, required browser CI, normal deployment and affected
public visitor journeys. Those stages remain separate. Test failures and
corrected assumptions are retained in the private checkpoint. Human reader
feedback is not supplied by these engineering checks.

The two Rodriguez photographs remain documentary context. Original creator
pages and their CC BY 2.0 links were inspected on October 10, 2026. The second
creator caption gives an approximate area near Banner Road and 15th Street.
No exact camera coordinates, bearing, calibrated historical time or continuous
appearance interval follows from that caption. The first historical appearance
interval remains a separate required outcome.
