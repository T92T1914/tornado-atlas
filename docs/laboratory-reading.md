current dossiers. Selecting a reference changes the nearby evidence and reading
links. It preserves the experiment settings. No selection supplies a historical
shape, wind field, physical size, camera pose or damage sequence.

The reference panel displays one existing reviewed public image with its source,
creator roles, item rights, version and limits. It checks the current archive
association, received image bytes and decoded dimensions before showing the
image. This establishes agreement with the published record, not independent
authenticity or permission. Full images keep their proportions and remain linked
to the complete published file. A failed image has a retry and retains article
and source routes.

After you choose a model control, canvas or experiment link, late reference
updates preserve the current visible interaction's vertical position. The
experiment link uses the same preservation during a pointer press or keyboard
focus. Scrolling, moving focus away, changing the reference or leaving the
document ends that position preservation. The
reference remains optional and does not change model values or start motion.

## Continuous comparison with background wind

The continuous reference describes the same prescribed Rankine field as the
sampled experiment. It does not solve fluid dynamics or validate a historical
tornado or structure. Its finite window remains the center moving from -6 R to
+6 R. Wind comparison duration includes equality. Component-capacity duration
requires a strict exceedance.

With eastward background speed B, peak swirl V and signed northward offset beta,
let q be squared along-track distance in core-radius units. Inside the core,
squared speed is `(B - V * beta)^2 + V^2 * q`. Outside it is
`B^2 + (V^2 - 2 * B * V * beta) / (q + beta^2)`. The reference splits the
finite path at the core, evaluates extrema and solves the threshold intervals
on each piece. Constant fields, tangencies and strict equality need their own
controls. Arithmetic outside its representable domain is refused.

This comparison can reveal a maximum or brief capacity exceedance missed by the
481 instants. It does not change the sampled component's failure rule, provide
an exact physical failure time or establish a general sampling error bound.

## Historical likeness remains an evidence question

No current dossier supports a complete historical three-dimensional likeness or
continuous registered appearance. El Reno has the strongest admitted discrete
appearance relationships. Tuscaloosa's published figure supports comparison of
particular views, with its stated estimated times and foreground obstruction.
Damage photographs and warnings from the other dossiers cannot substitute for
storm-time optical constraints.

The [2025 Gaussian-splatting laboratory paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.18677v1)
uses frames selected at a common time step from eight chamber-camera recordings, with camera
initialization and manual processing limits. It remains a conditional method
lead. No laboratory dataset, independent data rights or historical Atlas result
is established by citing that paper. The
[2018 cloud photogrammetry paper](https://amt.copernicus.org/articles/11/593/2018/)
uses prior calibration, stereo constraints and an independent comparison in a
different application. Its publisher abstract informs the constraint question;
it is not validation of the Atlas renderer.

Historical reconstruction still requires a source-specific question, useful
independent timing or camera constraints, and comparisons capable of rejecting a
fit. Unknown intervals remain unknown.
