THE HISTORICAL STORM COLLECTION

Tornado history, through the surviving record.

Four exhibits follow storms through the forecasts, the damage and the people who lived through them. Read an account, inspect its sources, or explore what can be placed on a historical clock. Each event has a different surviving record.

OKLAHOMA · MAY 31, 2013

El Reno

A changing course and a circulation larger than one visible funnel. Follow the warning record, photographs, published path and the accounts of eight people who died.

Layers & help

Modern geographic context, not historical roads or imagery. Markers show reported start positions or labeled end fallback. A numbered group counts source records, not unique storms or risk. Its symbol summarizes the visible records, not an observed tornado position.

Scroll to zoom on desktop. Use arrows, + and − when the map has focus. On a narrow screen, use zoom buttons and enable dragging when needed. The list provides the same records without marker hunting.

Known disputed positions are excluded from ordinary markers and fitted views. Their original values remain in the record, with an explicit map action.
