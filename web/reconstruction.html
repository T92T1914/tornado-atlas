WHAT IS RECORDED

The historical track.

The NWS source supplies 39 minute positions and the center path. The moving marker passes through those positions, with linear interpolation between them. The clock labels which you are seeing. Published times have minute precision and no supplied error bounds.

WHAT STILL NEEDS EVIDENCE

The appearance of the storm.

A center path does not give the funnel's changing dimensions, rain curtains or subvortex motion. Those need registered photographs or footage. This scene therefore does not claim to reproduce what a particular witness saw, or show when a building failed.
