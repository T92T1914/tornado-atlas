Read the illustration’s limits.

A visible funnel is not the whole circulation.

NOAA explains that a tornado can reach the ground without a visible condensation funnel. In seven radar-observed cases, Houser and colleagues found generally non-descending development, with exceptions and sensitivity to how the vortex signature was defined. Neither finding establishes one sequence every tornado follows.

This study chooses one pathway. Assumed circulation strengthens before a continuous condensation body becomes visible. Dust appears separately, and the cloud thins and erodes during weakening. The timing, opacity, shape and arrow field are authored assumptions. No atmospheric equations, humidity or physical wind speeds are solved here.
