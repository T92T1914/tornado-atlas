WHAT YOU ARE SEEING

A visible form is only part of the storm.

The condensation funnel, dust and debris help make a tornado visible. Its appearance alone does not establish its wind speed or damage rating. The same illustrated form here can be shown with a shorter visible funnel.

WHERE THIS FITS

From a form study to a historical view.

This drawing uses repeatable particle patterns and adjustable geometry. It does not solve the air flow or estimate damage. The rotation rate, cloud shape and ground grid are artistic choices.

A reconstruction of a particular tornado will need registered camera views, dates, positions and measured constraints. The El Reno research notebook tracks that work separately. The survey gallery shows real documented damage.
