What this collection can establish

These are source cases, not a count of unique physical storms or complete national coverage. Reporting criteria and source revisions matter when comparing years. F and JEF are separate damage scales. Rating intervals do not become measured wind speeds.

Timezone, coordinate datum and the separate wind field's unit are unresolved in this adapter. Reported coordinates remain readable without being placed on an assumed basemap. Shared casualty and building values are not added into totals.
