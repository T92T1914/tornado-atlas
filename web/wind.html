THE MODEL

A useful shape, with deliberate limits.

Inside the core, swirl speed rises linearly from zero. Outside, it falls as the inverse of distance. This is a Rankine vortex: a simple analytic velocity field. It has no inflow, updraft, terrain, gusts, multiple vortices, or evolving storm structure.

Arrow direction shows the horizontal flow. The pressure readout is dynamic pressure, not the atmospheric pressure deficit inside a tornado. Tracers start paused and pause again when the page is hidden.

FROM WIND TO DAMAGE

Force is only one part of failure.

The component experiment adds one explicit capacity rule to the drag calculation. A real damage model also needs building geometry, connections, materials, internal pressure and debris impacts. This experiment cannot assign an EF rating, reconstruct a building failure or establish structural safety.

Explore the El Reno survey photographs for documented damage. NIST's tornado design research uses much more extensive load and resistance modeling; this simple experiment has not been validated against it.
