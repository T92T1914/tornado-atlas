MAINLAND CHINA, 2003 TO 2019

China: source records.

Read the tornado cases of a research database for mainland China, with their recorded dates, times, positions and ratings. Each case keeps the database's own label.

What this collection can establish

These are source cases from version 2 of a research database, not a complete count of tornadoes in China. Each position is the authors' geocoded point for a place description. It is not a survey position or a path, so no case is drawn on the map.

A date range gives the approximate dates of occurrence, not how long the tornado lasted. Times are UTC+8 as recorded, and words such as afternoon stay as they were written. No time is converted to UTC.

The database says its place names, time notes and damage descriptions come from the Yearbook of Meteorological Disasters in China. The yearbook's own terms have not been read, so those columns are left out here. Ratings are shown as the database reports them and do not become wind speeds.
